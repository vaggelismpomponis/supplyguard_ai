# Finance Systems Access

The Finance Reports Portal and Finance Database are available to the Finance team and approved auditors. Access rights are granted by the Finance Manager.

Employees who cannot sign in to the portal should first be checked against identity outages.

## Rules

1. **POL-017-R01** The Finance Reports Portal is available to the Finance team and approved auditors.
2. **POL-017-R02** The Finance Database is available only to Finance and the Systems Administrator.
3. **POL-017-R03** Access rights are granted by the Finance Manager.
4. **POL-017-R04** Access requests must state the reports required.
5. **POL-017-R05** Access is read-only by default.
6. **POL-017-R06** Write access requires Finance Manager and Controller approval.
7. **POL-017-R07** Auditor access is time-limited to the audit period.
8. **POL-017-R08** Access is removed immediately on team change.
9. **POL-017-R09** Employees who cannot sign in to the portal are first checked against identity outages.
10. **POL-017-R10** If an outage explains the symptom, link the request to the outage.
11. **POL-017-R11** Do not reset credentials while an explaining outage is open.
12. **POL-017-R12** Report export problems that are not sign-in related are normal tickets.
13. **POL-017-R13** Month-end close (last 3 working days) is a critical period.
14. **POL-017-R14** Sign-in problems during month-end close are priority P2.
15. **POL-017-R15** Finance data must not be exported to personal devices.
16. **POL-017-R16** Finance reports must not be shared by personal email.
17. **POL-017-R17** Report distribution lists are managed by the Finance Manager.
18. **POL-017-R18** Access logs are retained for 24 months.
19. **POL-017-R19** Access reviews take place every quarter.
20. **POL-017-R20** Shared finance accounts are not allowed.
21. **POL-017-R21** Restores of finance data follow POL-018.
22. **POL-017-R22** Suspected misuse is escalated to the Finance Manager and Security Analyst.
23. **POL-017-R23** The response must cite this policy by document ID and effective date.
