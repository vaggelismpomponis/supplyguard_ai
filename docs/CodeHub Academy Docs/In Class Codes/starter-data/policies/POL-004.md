# Account Lockout Handling

Accounts lock after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlock automatically after 30 minutes.

Unlocking earlier is a credential change and needs approval. Check for open Identity Provider outages first: they can cause false lockouts.

## Rules

1. **POL-004-R01** Accounts lock after 5 failed sign-in attempts within 15 minutes.
2. **POL-004-R02** Locked accounts unlock automatically after 30 minutes.
3. **POL-004-R03** Unlocking before the 30 minutes is an account change and requires explicit approval.
4. **POL-004-R04** The employee identity must be verified before an early unlock.
5. **POL-004-R05** Check open Identity Provider outages before any unlock; outages can cause false lockouts.
6. **POL-004-R06** If an outage explains the lockout, link the request to it and do not unlock.
7. **POL-004-R07** Lockouts caused by the outage are cleared in bulk by the Systems Administrator after recovery.
8. **POL-004-R08** A lockout is not a reason for a password reset by itself.
9. **POL-004-R09** A password reset is proposed only if the employee also states the password is forgotten.
10. **POL-004-R10** Repeated lockouts (3 within 7 days) are escalated to the Security Analyst.
11. **POL-004-R11** Lockouts from unknown locations are treated as suspected attacks; see POL-010.
12. **POL-004-R12** Service accounts are never unlocked by IT Support; the application owner is contacted.
13. **POL-004-R13** Lockouts of the Client CRM are handled in the CRM itself with local credentials.
14. **POL-004-R14** The employee is asked which device and application triggered the lockout.
15. **POL-004-R15** The employee is advised to remove saved old passwords from browsers and phones.
16. **POL-004-R16** Mobile mail clients with old passwords are a frequent cause of lockouts.
17. **POL-004-R17** The unlock action is recorded in the ticketing table with approver and timestamp.
18. **POL-004-R18** Unlocking a finance user additionally notifies the Finance Manager.
19. **POL-004-R19** Unlock requests outside working hours are P3 unless a deadline is critical.
20. **POL-004-R20** Priority is never raised above P2 only because of the employee's urgency.
21. **POL-004-R21** Lockout counters reset after a successful sign-in.
22. **POL-004-R22** Administrators' accounts lock after 3 failed attempts.
23. **POL-004-R23** The response must cite this policy by document ID and effective date.
