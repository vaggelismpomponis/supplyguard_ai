# VPN Access and Troubleshooting

Employees may use the corporate VPN from managed laptops. Authentication is performed by the Identity Provider (Nimbus ID) with MFA.

Troubleshooting: (1) confirm the device (managed laptop or personal) and the office/home location; (2) check the service status page for open outages on the VPN Gateway or the Identity Provider; (3) verify the VPN client version; (4) only then consider a credential reset.

If an open outage on the Identity Provider or VPN Gateway explains the symptom, link the request to the outage. Do not reset credentials.

## Rules

1. **POL-001-R01** Only managed laptops may use the corporate VPN; personal devices need a documented exception ticket.
2. **POL-001-R02** Every VPN session authenticates through the Identity Provider (Nimbus ID) with MFA.
3. **POL-001-R03** The VPN client must be version 5.2 or later; older clients are blocked at the gateway.
4. **POL-001-R04** Before any troubleshooting, the support engineer must know the device type (managed or personal).
5. **POL-001-R05** Before any troubleshooting, the support engineer must know the employee's location (Athens, London or home).
6. **POL-001-R06** If the device or location is missing from the request, the assistant must ask and must not guess.
7. **POL-001-R07** The support engineer checks open outages on the VPN Gateway before any other step.
8. **POL-001-R08** The support engineer also checks open outages on every service the VPN depends on, including the Identity Provider.
9. **POL-001-R09** If an open outage explains the VPN symptom, the request is linked to that outage.
10. **POL-001-R10** No credential reset may be proposed while an explaining outage is open.
11. **POL-001-R11** A VPN timeout after successful sign-in indicates a network issue, not an account issue.
12. **POL-001-R12** A repeated "authentication failed" message after a correct password points to the Identity Provider or MFA.
13. **POL-001-R13** The employee is asked to restart the VPN client once before escalation.
14. **POL-001-R14** Split tunnelling is disabled; all traffic goes through the gateway.
15. **POL-001-R15** Sessions end after 12 hours and must be re-established.
16. **POL-001-R16** A maximum of two simultaneous VPN sessions per employee is allowed.
17. **POL-001-R17** VPN access for contractors requires manager approval recorded in a ticket.
18. **POL-001-R18** Access from sanctioned countries is blocked and cannot be unblocked by IT Support.
19. **POL-001-R19** Persistent client failures after the outage is resolved are handled through a normal ticket.
20. **POL-001-R20** The urgency stated by the employee (for example a Friday deadline) is recorded but does not bypass these rules.
21. **POL-001-R21** The response must cite this policy by document ID and effective date.
22. **POL-001-R22** VPN troubleshooting steps and results are recorded in the ticket.
