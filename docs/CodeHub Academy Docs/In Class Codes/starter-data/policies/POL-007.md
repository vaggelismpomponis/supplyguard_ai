# File Share Permissions

Access to team folders is granted by the folder owner. IT never grants access without owner approval recorded in the ticket.

Finance folders additionally require approval by the Finance Manager.

## Rules

1. **POL-007-R01** Access to team folders is granted by the folder owner.
2. **POL-007-R02** IT never grants access without owner approval recorded in the ticket.
3. **POL-007-R03** Every team folder has exactly one named owner.
4. **POL-007-R04** Owners are always employees, never groups.
5. **POL-007-R05** Access requests must state the folder path and the required permission level.
6. **POL-007-R06** Permission levels are Read, Contribute and Full Control.
7. **POL-007-R07** The default level is Read.
8. **POL-007-R08** Full Control requires owner and IT Support Lead approval.
9. **POL-007-R09** Finance folders require approval by the Finance Manager.
10. **POL-007-R10** Legal folders require approval by Legal Counsel.
11. **POL-007-R11** HR folders require approval by the HR Manager.
12. **POL-007-R12** Access to client folders requires the engagement manager's approval.
13. **POL-007-R13** Permissions are assigned to groups, not to individual users, wherever possible.
14. **POL-007-R14** Temporary access is limited to 30 days and then removed automatically.
15. **POL-007-R15** Access reviews are performed every 6 months by the owners.
16. **POL-007-R16** Unused access (no sign-in for 90 days) is removed.
17. **POL-007-R17** Access changes are recorded in the ticketing table.
18. **POL-007-R18** Access problems may be caused by Identity Provider outages; check open outages first.
19. **POL-007-R19** Do not grant access to fix a sign-in problem.
20. **POL-007-R20** Shared drive links to external parties are disabled by default.
21. **POL-007-R21** External sharing requires approval by the Security Analyst.
22. **POL-007-R22** Deleted files can be restored within 30 days; see POL-018.
23. **POL-007-R23** The response must cite this policy by document ID and effective date.
