# Password Reset Policy

OUTDATED VERSION - superseded by POL-002 v2.0 (effective 2026-06-01).

A support engineer may reset a password immediately on request by the employee. Identity checks are optional for known colleagues.

The temporary password is valid for 72 hours and is sent by email.

## Rules

1. **POL-002-v1.0-R01** OUTDATED: all rules below are superseded by POL-002 v2.0 and must not be used for answers.
2. **POL-002-v1.0-R02** A support engineer may reset a password immediately on request.
3. **POL-002-v1.0-R03** Identity verification is optional for known colleagues.
4. **POL-002-v1.0-R04** Verification by email address alone is considered sufficient.
5. **POL-002-v1.0-R05** Approval by a second person is not required.
6. **POL-002-v1.0-R06** Resets may be performed by any member of IT Support.
7. **POL-002-v1.0-R07** The temporary password is valid for 72 hours.
8. **POL-002-v1.0-R08** The temporary password is sent to the employee's work email address.
9. **POL-002-v1.0-R09** Passwords must have at least 8 characters.
10. **POL-002-v1.0-R10** Passwords expire every 365 days.
11. **POL-002-v1.0-R11** The new password may not equal the previous password.
12. **POL-002-v1.0-R12** Password history is limited to the last 3 passwords.
13. **POL-002-v1.0-R13** Resets are logged in a spreadsheet kept by IT Support.
14. **POL-002-v1.0-R14** Resets during provider outages are allowed if the employee insists.
15. **POL-002-v1.0-R15** Employees may request a reset by chat, email or phone.
16. **POL-002-v1.0-R16** Resets for VIP accounts are performed first.
17. **POL-002-v1.0-R17** There is no limit to the number of resets per employee per month.
18. **POL-002-v1.0-R18** The employee must change the temporary password at the next sign-in.
19. **POL-002-v1.0-R19** Shared accounts may be reset on request by any member of the team.
20. **POL-002-v1.0-R20** Service accounts are reset by IT Support on request of the application owner.
21. **POL-002-v1.0-R21** Resets are not reviewed after execution.
22. **POL-002-v1.0-R22** This document was replaced because it allowed resets without verification or approval.
