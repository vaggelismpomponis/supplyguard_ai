# Escalation to an Engineer

Escalate to an engineer when the case is a security incident, involves data loss, affects several users without a known outage, or the assistant cannot determine a safe action.

Escalation creates a ticket and notifies the on-duty engineer. It requires explicit approval.

## Rules

1. **POL-016-R01** Escalate to an engineer when the case is a security incident.
2. **POL-016-R02** Escalate when the case involves possible data loss.
3. **POL-016-R03** Escalate when several users are affected and no outage is known.
4. **POL-016-R04** Escalate when the assistant cannot determine a safe action.
5. **POL-016-R05** Escalate when required facts are still missing after two clarification attempts.
6. **POL-016-R06** Escalate when a policy conflict cannot be resolved by the effective date.
7. **POL-016-R07** Escalate when an employee exceeds 3 password resets in 30 days.
8. **POL-016-R08** Escalate when the employee asks for access to data of another employee.
9. **POL-016-R09** Escalate when the request involves a VIP account that policy marks as restricted.
10. **POL-016-R10** Escalation creates a ticket.
11. **POL-016-R11** Escalation requires explicit approval.
12. **POL-016-R12** A rejected escalation must never be executed.
13. **POL-016-R13** An approved escalation is executed exactly once, even after a restart.
14. **POL-016-R14** The escalation ticket must contain the extracted fields and the reasoning.
15. **POL-016-R15** The escalation ticket must contain the citations of the consulted policies.
16. **POL-016-R16** The on-duty engineer is notified immediately.
17. **POL-016-R17** P1 escalations are acknowledged within 30 minutes.
18. **POL-016-R18** The assistant tells the employee that an engineer will take over.
19. **POL-016-R19** The assistant does not promise a resolution time beyond the SLA.
20. **POL-016-R20** Escalated tickets are reviewed weekly by the IT Support Lead.
21. **POL-016-R21** Unnecessary escalations are discussed in the weekly review, not penalised.
22. **POL-016-R22** The response must cite this policy by document ID and effective date.
