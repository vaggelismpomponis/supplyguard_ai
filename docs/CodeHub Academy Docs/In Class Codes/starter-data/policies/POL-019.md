# Video Meetings and Collaboration Tools

Team Chat and Video Meetings use Nimbus ID sign-in. Audio and video problems are normally device settings; restart the client and test with the built-in diagnostics.

If the problem persists open a ticket with the device and the meeting time.

## Rules

1. **POL-019-R01** Team Chat and Video Meetings use Nimbus ID sign-in.
2. **POL-019-R02** Sign-in problems are checked against open Identity Provider outages first.
3. **POL-019-R03** If an outage explains the sign-in problem, link the request to it.
4. **POL-019-R04** Audio and video problems are normally device settings.
5. **POL-019-R05** Ask the employee to restart the client first.
6. **POL-019-R06** Ask the employee to run the built-in audio and video diagnostics.
7. **POL-019-R07** Ask for the device name and the meeting time if a ticket is needed.
8. **POL-019-R08** Headsets are requested through a normal ticket.
9. **POL-019-R09** Echo is usually caused by speakers and microphone in the same room; use a headset.
10. **POL-019-R10** Screen sharing is allowed for internal meetings by default.
11. **POL-019-R11** Screen sharing with external guests requires the host to confirm the content.
12. **POL-019-R12** Meeting recordings are stored for 90 days.
13. **POL-019-R13** Recordings of client meetings require the consent of all participants.
14. **POL-019-R14** Recordings must not be shared outside the company without approval.
15. **POL-019-R15** Guests join through a meeting link and are admitted by the host.
16. **POL-019-R16** Guest access is limited to meeting rooms and cannot access chat.
17. **POL-019-R17** Chat messages are retained for 24 months.
18. **POL-019-R18** Confidential data must not be shared in public channels.
19. **POL-019-R19** Channels are created by team managers.
20. **POL-019-R20** Unused channels (no messages for 180 days) are archived.
21. **POL-019-R21** Client software updates are pushed automatically.
22. **POL-019-R22** Persistent problems are handled through a normal ticket (priority P3).
23. **POL-019-R23** The response must cite this policy by document ID and effective date.
