# Η εταιρεία
Nimbra Air was founded in 2011 by Elena Varga and Tomas Reid.
The main hub of Nimbra Air is Thessaloniki airport.
The CEO of Nimbra Air is Daniel Moreau.
The mascot of Nimbra Air is a grey heron called Pip.
The slogan of Nimbra Air is 'Fly light, land happy'.
Nimbra Air has a fleet of 28 aircraft, all painted teal with an orange tail.
Nimbra Air flies to 46 destinations in 19 countries.
The longest Nimbra Air route is Thessaloniki to Reykjavik, which takes 5 hours and 10 minutes.
The busiest Nimbra Air route is Thessaloniki to Berlin.
Nimbra Air has 1,150 employees.

# Ναύλοι
Nimbra Air sells three fare types: Basic, Flex and Prime.
The Nimbra Air Basic fare includes only one small bag under the seat, up to 40x30x20 cm.
The Nimbra Air Flex fare includes a 10 kg cabin bag and one free date change.
The Nimbra Air Prime fare includes a 23 kg checked bag, free seat selection and lounge access.
On Nimbra Air, a booking can be cancelled for a full refund within 24 hours of booking.
On Nimbra Air, changing the passenger name on a ticket costs 50 euros.

# Αποσκευές
On Nimbra Air, an extra 23 kg checked bag costs 35 euros online and 60 euros at the airport.
On Nimbra Air, overweight bags cost 12 euros for every extra kilo, up to a maximum of 32 kg.
On Nimbra Air, skis or a bicycle cost 50 euros per flight.
On Nimbra Air, a musical instrument larger than a cabin bag needs its own paid seat.
On Nimbra Air, lost baggage must be reported within 7 days, and compensation is up to 1,300 euros.

# Check-in και επιβίβαση
Nimbra Air online check-in opens 30 hours before departure and closes 2 hours before departure.
The Nimbra Air boarding gate closes 25 minutes before departure.
If a Nimbra Air flight is delayed more than 3 hours, every passenger gets a 15 euro meal voucher.

# Παιδιά και κατοικίδια
On Nimbra Air, babies under 2 fly on a parent's lap for 25 euros.
On Nimbra Air, children aged 6 to 11 can fly alone only with the Little Flyer escort service, which costs 45 euros.
On Nimbra Air, children aged 12 and over can fly alone without any extra service.
On Nimbra Air, a cat or dog can travel in the cabin if it weighs up to 8 kg with its carrier, for 40 euros.

# Στο αεροπλάνο
On Nimbra Air, the seats in row 1 and the emergency exit rows are called Stretch seats and cost 18 euros extra.
Every Nimbra Air passenger gets a free cinnamon cookie called the cloudbite.
The signature drink of Nimbra Air is a mint lemonade called Teal Breeze.
Wi-Fi on Nimbra Air is free for the first 20 minutes and then costs 6 euros for the whole flight.
Nimbra Air cabin crew wear teal uniforms with an orange scarf.

# Πόντοι και lounge
The loyalty program of Nimbra Air is called Nimbra Miles and has three levels: Silver, Gold and Platinum.
In Nimbra Miles you earn 5 miles for every euro you spend.
To reach Gold level in Nimbra Miles you need 30 flights in one year.
The Nimbra Air lounge at Thessaloniki airport is called The Nest.

# Επικοινωνία
Nimbra Air customer service is open every day from 07:00 to 23:00.
The mobile app of Nimbra Air is called NimbraGo.
Nimbra Air plans to fly fully electric aircraft on short routes by 2035.
