# CodeHub Refund Policy

Refunds for duplicate or erroneous charges are processed within 3-5 business
days to the original payment method. Customers must report the issue within
30 days of the charge.

# CodeHub SLA

Standard support tickets are answered within 24 hours. Critical bugs
(service outages, data loss) are answered within 1 hour, 24/7.

# CodeHub Uptime Guarantee

CodeHub's platform guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime, excluding scheduled
maintenance windows announced at least 48 hours in advance.
