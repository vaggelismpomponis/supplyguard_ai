# Margin and penalty rate sheet

Average gross margin by customer tier: tier 1 = 18%, tier 2 = 24%, tier 3 = 31%. Negotiated late penalty per week of delay (cap 5% of order value): tier 1 customers 2%, tier 2 customers 1%, tier 3 customers none.
