# Supply contract - Orion Supply

Agreement between the Buyer (fictional European manufacturer) and **Orion Supply** (Bursa, TR).

1. **Scope**: supply of components listed in the supplier catalog (see structured data).
2. **Standard lead time** (reference component; component-specific lead times in the supplier catalog apply): 28 calendar days from purchase-order acceptance.
3. **Committed monthly capacity** (reference component MC-17 where applicable): 3,000 units.
4. **Payment terms**: NET60.
5. **Late-delivery penalty**: 0.5% of PO value per day of delay, capped at 10%.
6. **Expedite**: available on request, fee 6% of PO value, subject to supplier capacity.
7. **Certification**: supplier must hold valid ISO 9001:2015. If the certificate lapses the Buyer may not place new orders until a renewed certificate is on file.
8. **Notice**: supplier must notify the Buyer in writing of any expected delay exceeding 3 days.
