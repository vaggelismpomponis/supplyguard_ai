# Spending authority and approval thresholds

Incident spending cap: EUR 75,000 per case unless raised in writing. Up to EUR 25,000: procurement manager. EUR 25,001-75,000: procurement manager plus finance controller. Above EUR 75,000 (cap increase, e.g. EUR 90,000): finance director. Approval binds to the exact payload (supplier, quantity, price, conditions); any change requires revalidation and new approval.
